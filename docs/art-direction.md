# Redline prototype art direction

## Thesis

Protection is introduced through the material and resolved through the seating application.
The experience borrows its composition from engineering studios and industrial product photography.
The firearm occupies only the opening test shot and has no brand, specification, interactive controls, or audio.

## System

The first frame pairs an original macro textile image with an oversized Barlow Condensed wordmark, Geist interface type, a narrow red rule, and a single filled action.
Dark graphite surfaces, warm white typography, silver material highlights, and restrained signal red form the semantic token system in `src/app/globals.css`.
The brighter red text token preserves legibility at small sizes while the original signal red remains the primary action and technical-line color.
Company identity is an original simple three-stripe mark rather than a tactical symbol.

## Motion

The motion’s purpose is explanation and spatial continuity in an occasional marketing experience.
GSAP owns the title introduction and long scene timeline.
Lenis is the only smooth-scroll engine.
Motion handles the small product-description opacity transition, while CSS owns hover and press feedback.
Interactive feedback uses a 160–180 ms duration; chapter jumps respond immediately.
The title introduction is finite, with no perpetual ambient animation that would require a pause control.

The camera changes perspective between establishment, travel, glass, upholstery, textile macro, impact, orbit, assembly, explosion, and finished seat.
The story is completely reversible.
Material deformation follows an illustrative wave, not a physics solver or test model.
The use of separate models preserves replacement boundaries without building a permanent procedural asset pipeline.

## Scope

The deliverable is a working concept prototype.
The highest-priority future inputs are approved product geometry, approved textile appearance, verified construction, verified claims, a public domain, and a contact endpoint.
No awards or recognition are implied by the requested visual quality bar.
